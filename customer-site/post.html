Three weeks across the Western Ghats

The monsoon was supposed to be over by the time I got to Coorg, but the hills didn't get the memo. Most mornings started with fog thick enough that the coffee estates looked like they'd been pencilled in and then half-erased.

The bus to Sakleshpur runs twice a day and the second one leaves without much warning. I know this because I watched it pull away from the stand while asking a shopkeeper for directions, and then spent four hours finding out what the town looks like on foot instead.

If you're planning something similar: carry cash, confirm the second bus time with at least two people, and don't trust the fog to lift before 10am.
